1. Overview

Mzansi TV ("the App") is an Android application providing live streaming access to public broadcast television channels in South Africa. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data in compliance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Developer Program policies.

2. Data Collection and Processing

A. On-Device Local Data

Your starred and favorite channels are stored entirely on your local device using Android Room SQLite database. This preference data is never uploaded, transferred, or shared with external servers.

B. Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

The App integrates Google AdMob to serve in-app advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device telemetry, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to infer coarse location such as country or city)
  • App interaction, ad impressions, and crash diagnostics to prevent fraud

For more details, review Google's Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to stream live HLS video feeds and load ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect Wi-Fi / cellular connectivity changes.

The App does NOT request location permissions, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

4. Consent Management (Google UMP)

Users in applicable regions can update or revoke advertising consent at any time via the in-app Privacy & Info dialog, or via Android Settings → Google → Ads.
