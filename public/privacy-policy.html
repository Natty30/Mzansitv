1. Introduction

Mzansi TV is a live television streaming application for Android providing access to South African public broadcast channels (SABC 1, SABC 2, SABC 3, SABC News, SABC Sport, and SABC Education). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information handled in connection with the app.

2. Information Collection and Use

Mzansi TV does not require user registration or logins. We do not collect names, phone numbers, or passwords.

  • Local Preferences: Channel favoriting is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database (AndroidX Room) and is never transmitted to external servers.
  • Streaming Data: Video content is streamed directly via standard HTTP Live Streaming (HLS) protocols over TLS.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Mzansi TV incorporates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to serve advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address (used to infer coarse geographic region)
  • Device performance, model, operating system version, and screen size
  • Ad interaction metrics and anti-fraud telemetry

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, review Google's official privacy policies at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Consent Management (Google UMP)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the ePrivacy Directive, and the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), we implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Users can manage or revoke their advertising consent anytime within the app or via their Android system settings.

5. Permissions

Mzansi TV requests only:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: To download video stream fragments and retrieve advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To monitor buffer connectivity health.

6. Data Security

All network communications use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption in transit. No personal user databases are maintained by Mzansi TV.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
